Frequently asked

A few things
you might wonder.

Still have a question? Send me a note and I’ll be happy to talk it through.

What kind of websites do you build?
I focus on professional business websites, restaurant websites, landing pages and redesigns. We can shape the scope around what your business needs.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes. My work is focused on helping businesses present themselves clearly online, including small and independent businesses.
Can you build responsive websites?
Yes. I design for phones, tablets and desktop screens, with layouts and interactions adapted for each size.
How does the project process work?
We start by understanding your business and goals, then plan the structure and visual direction, build and review the site, and prepare it for launch.
Can I provide my own domain and hosting?
Absolutely. You can use a domain and hosting you already have. We can also discuss practical deployment options that suit the website.
Can an existing website be redesigned?
Yes. I can help rethink its visual direction, structure and responsive experience so it better reflects your business today.
